      Notes from the studio

      About the project

      A Hybrid AI-Driven Framework for Localized Sinhala Virtual Brand Ambassadors

      ASVBA Sinhala is a research prototype that generates Sinhala-speaking AI brand ambassadors for digital marketing campaigns in Sri Lanka, using audio-visual synthesis.

      How a video is made

      1. 1
        Sinhala draft – a Sinhala language model (Llama-3-Sinhala) writes a first draft from your brief.
      2. 2
        Script polishing – Google Gemini turns the draft into a spoken Sinhala script and the on-screen text.
      3. 3
        Human approval – you read, correct and approve the script with your name.
      4. 4
        Sinhala voice – SinhalaVITS speaks the approved script.
      5. 5
        Talking ambassador – SadTalker animates the character and LatentSync 1.5 refines the lip movement.
      6. 6
        Ad composer – product photo, logo, Sinhala captions and the AI label are added in 9:16 and 16:9.

      Responsible AI

      • Every ambassador is AI-generated, and every video says so.
      • No voice or video is made before a person approves the script.
      • Only brand material the user is allowed to use.
      • Briefs are processed with Google Gemini – no confidential or personal data.
